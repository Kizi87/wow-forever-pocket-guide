# Professions — Blacksmithing + Mining

## Verdict

Keep **Blacksmithing + Mining**. Every Forever profession guide checked recommends it for a levelling Paladin (multi-source: topbuilds, hardcorehelper, classicwowforever.com, overgear, expcarry, gametyrant, conquestcapped). Reasons given: Blacksmithing makes bind-on-pickup plate/mail pieces for Paladins that only the crafter can wear, plus sharpening/weightstones for a 2H; Mining feeds it and sells. Endgame alternatives mentioned: Mining + Engineering (PvP/Prot), Herbalism + Alchemy (Holy). Not needed now.
